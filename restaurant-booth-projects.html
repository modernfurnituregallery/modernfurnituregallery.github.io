How a restaurant project works

From floor plan to opening day.

  1. Share your space

    Send your floor plan or measurements, or have us visit to discuss your layout.

  2. Pick materials & colours

    Choose shapes, stitching and commercial-grade vinyl, leather or fabric to match your brand.

  3. Built in Mississauga

    Your booths are manufactured in our Mississauga facility with a fast turnaround.

  4. Delivered across Ontario

    We deliver to your restaurant anywhere in Toronto, the GTA and Ontario.

Restaurant project questions

Which businesses have you built seating for?

Our clients include Crab Boil, Habibz Corner and Nikon, along with many other restaurants, cafés, bars and lounges across Toronto and the GTA.

Can you build booths for several locations?

Yes. We can build seating for a single restaurant or outfit several locations, and we keep your sizes, materials and colours on file so every location matches.

Can you match our brand colours and layout?

Yes. Every booth is built to your floor plan in any fabric, vinyl or leather, in any colour, with straight, L-shaped, U-shaped, curved or round layouts.

Can you fix or recover the booths we already have?

Yes. We reupholster existing booths, banquettes and chairs with commercial-grade, easy-clean materials. See our reupholstery services.
