Restaurant Booths for Sale in Ontario: Custom-Built vs. Ready-Made

By Modern Furniture Gallery, custom furniture makers in Mississauga since 2000 · November 11, 2026

If you are searching for restaurant booths for sale in Ontario, you will find three options: ready-made booths from a catalogue, used booths from another restaurant, and custom booths built for your space. Each can make sense. Here is how they compare, and what to check before you buy any of them.

Ready-made booths

Ready-made booths come in set lengths, heights and colours. If a standard size fits your walls and you like the stock finishes, they are a simple choice. The catch is that dining rooms rarely line up with catalogue sizes, so you can end up with gaps at the end of a run, a booth that blocks a door, or a colour that is close to your brand but not quite right.

Used booths

Used booths on marketplaces can look like a bargain. Before you buy, check the frame for loose joints, the seats for flattened foam and the upholstery for cracks and tears. Make sure the sizes really work for your layout, because cutting a booth down is rarely practical. If the frame is solid but the cover is tired, reupholstery can give used booths a new look in your colours.

Custom-built booths

Custom booths are built to your floor plan, seat count and brand. Runs fit wall to wall, corners and L-shapes fit the room, and you choose the exact colour, stitching and material. Because we manufacture in Mississauga and sell factory-direct, custom booths are often closer in price to ready-made than people expect, and every booth is quoted to your design.

What to check before you buy any booth

  • Measurements: seat height, table height, seat depth and overall depth (see our booth sizing guide)
  • Construction: ask what the frame is made of and whether it is built for commercial use
  • Upholstery: commercial-grade vinyl or leather is easiest to keep clean
  • Comfort: sit on a sample, because seat foam and back angle make a big difference
  • Delivery and timing: confirm the date against your opening or renovation schedule
  • Aftercare: can the booths be reupholstered later?
