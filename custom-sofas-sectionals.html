How custom works

From the first visit to delivery, we keep it simple.

  1. Visit or call

    Come to our Mississauga showroom or call us to talk through your space, style and budget.

  2. Choose your design

    Pick the style, size, shape, fabric or leather and colour. Every detail is made to order.

  3. We build it in Mississauga

    Your piece is handcrafted in Canada from premium materials, most within 1–3 weeks.

  4. Delivered to your door

    We deliver across Toronto, the GTA and Ontario, ready to enjoy.

Custom sofa questions

How much does a custom sofa cost?

Every sofa is made to order, so pricing depends on the size, design and fabric or leather you choose. Because we build in Mississauga and sell factory-direct at wholesaler prices, there is no retail markup. Contact us or visit the showroom for a quote.

How long does a custom sofa or sectional take?

Most custom pieces are completed within 1–3 weeks depending on the design and fabric selected.

Can you build a sectional to fit my room?

Yes. We build modular, L-shaped and chaise sectionals to your exact measurements, so the sectional fits your space, not the other way around.

What fabrics and colours can I choose?

Any fabric, leather and colour, including velvet and bouclé. Visit our Mississauga showroom to see and feel the options.

Do you deliver custom sofas in Toronto and the GTA?

Yes. We deliver across Toronto, the GTA and the rest of Ontario.
