Reupholster or Replace? A Guide for Sofas, Chairs and Restaurant Booths

By Modern Furniture Gallery, custom furniture makers in Mississauga since 2000 · November 11, 2026

Worn fabric, a torn vinyl seat or a colour that no longer fits the room does not always mean the furniture has to go. Reupholstery replaces the cover, and often the padding, while keeping the frame. Here is how to decide.

Reupholster when…

  • The frame is solid: no cracks, wobbles or broken joints
  • You love the shape and size, or it fits a space perfectly
  • It is a well-made or sentimental piece worth keeping
  • You want a new colour or fabric to match a new room or a new brand
  • It is fixed seating, like restaurant booths, that would be costly to remove and replace

Replace when…

  • The frame is broken or badly warped
  • You need a different size or shape
  • The piece was inexpensive to begin with and recovering it would cost more than it is worth

Restaurant booths and commercial seating

In a restaurant, torn or cracked vinyl looks bad to guests and is harder to keep clean. Reupholstering booths, banquettes and chairs in commercial-grade, easy-clean vinyl or leather refreshes the dining room without a full renovation, and is a simple way to roll out new brand colours. We plan the work with you to keep disruption to your service as low as possible.

Sofas, chairs and headboards at home

A good sofa frame can outlast several covers. Reupholstery lets you keep a piece you love and give it a new fabric, from velvet to bouclé to leather, often for a fraction of the cost of a comparable new piece. We also reupholster accent chairs and headboards.

How reupholstery works with us

  1. Send photos of the piece, or bring it to our Mississauga showroom.
  2. Choose your new fabric, vinyl or leather and colour.
  3. We give you a quote and timeline.
  4. We reupholster it in our workshop and return it ready to use.

Learn more on our reupholstery page. If replacing makes more sense, we can build a custom sofa or new restaurant booths instead.

Quick answers

Is it cheaper to reupholster or replace restaurant booths?

When the frames are solid, reupholstering booths is usually much cheaper than replacing them, and it avoids a full renovation.

Do you reupholster furniture in Mississauga?

Yes. Modern Furniture Gallery reupholsters sofas, chairs, headboards and restaurant booths at its Mississauga workshop for customers across Toronto and the GTA.
