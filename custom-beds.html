A custom bed is built around your room, not a catalogue. Choose the mattress size, the headboard height and style, and the fabric and colour, and we build it in our Mississauga workshop.

How custom works

From the first visit to delivery, we keep it simple.

  1. Visit or call

    Come to our Mississauga showroom or call us to talk through your space, style and budget.

  2. Choose your design

    Pick the style, size, shape, fabric or leather and colour. Every detail is made to order.

  3. We build it in Mississauga

    Your piece is handcrafted in Canada from premium materials, most within 1–3 weeks.

  4. Delivered to your door

    We deliver across Toronto, the GTA and Ontario, ready to enjoy.

Custom bed questions

Can you make a bed in a custom size?

Yes. Every bed is made to order, so we build the frame and headboard to the mattress size and headboard height you want, including oversized and extra-tall headboards.

What headboard styles can you make?

Tufted with buttons or crystals, channel-stitch, vertical panels, wingback and plain upholstered headboards, in any fabric, velvet or leather and any colour.

Can you make a matching bench or ottoman?

Yes. We can make a matching tufted bench, storage ottoman or accent chair in the same fabric so your bedroom is finished as a set.

Can you reupholster my existing headboard?

Yes. We reupholster headboards and bed frames in new fabric for a fraction of the cost of replacing them. See our reupholstery services.

Do you deliver custom beds in Toronto and the GTA?

Yes. Beds are made in Mississauga and delivered across Toronto, the GTA and Ontario.
