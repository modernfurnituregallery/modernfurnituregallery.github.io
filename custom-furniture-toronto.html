Sized for Toronto condos and restaurants

Toronto spaces are rarely standard. We build apartment-size sofas, deep sectionals for family rooms and modular pieces that come apart for tight elevators and hallways, all measured to your room.

For restaurants, cafés and bars we build custom booths and banquettes to your floor plan in commercial-grade vinyl, leather or fabric, and reupholster worn seating so your space looks new without a full renovation.

Toronto customer questions

Do you deliver custom furniture to Toronto?

Yes. Every piece is built in our Mississauga workshop and delivered to homes, condos and businesses across Toronto, including Downtown, Etobicoke, North York, Scarborough and East York.

Can you make a sofa or sectional that fits a Toronto condo?

Yes. Because everything is made to order, we size sofas and sectionals to your exact room, and modular sectionals can be built in sections that are easier to bring through condo elevators and hallways. Send us your measurements and we will help you plan it.

How long does custom furniture take?

Most custom pieces are ready in about 1 to 3 weeks, depending on the design and fabric. We will confirm your timeline when you order.

Can I see fabrics and finishes before I order?

Yes. Visit our showroom at 7025 Tomken Rd #19 in Mississauga, a short drive from Toronto off Highways 401 and 410, to see and feel fabrics, leathers and finished pieces.

Do you make restaurant booths for Toronto restaurants?

Yes. We design and build custom restaurant booths, banquettes and commercial seating for restaurants, cafés and bars across Toronto and the GTA, and we reupholster existing booths.
