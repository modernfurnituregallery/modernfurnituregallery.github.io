Banquette Seating for Restaurants: A Design Guide

By Modern Furniture Gallery, custom furniture makers in Mississauga since 2000 · November 11, 2026

A banquette is a long upholstered bench, usually set against a wall, with tables in front of it. Where a booth has seating on both sides of a table, a banquette lines one side and pairs with chairs on the other. Done well, banquette seating adds seats, defines the room and gives guests the comfortable spot they ask for when they book.

Where banquettes work best

  • Along a long wall, with a row of two-top tables and chairs on the aisle side
  • Under windows, where a booth back would block the view
  • In corners, as an L-shaped banquette that turns dead space into seating
  • Around columns, as a wraparound banquette
  • As a curved or round island in the middle of a lounge

Typical banquette sizes

These are common starting points. Because banquettes are built to order, we adjust them to your tables and the look you want.

Seat heightabout 18 in (46 cm), to suit standard 30 in (76 cm) tables
Seat depthabout 18–20 in (46–51 cm)
Back heightabout 36–42 in (91–107 cm) from the floor; taller for privacy or drama
Width per guestabout 24 in (61 cm)

Choosing a back style

Channel-stitch backs, with vertical channels, look clean and modern and suit most dining rooms. Button-tufted backs feel classic and upscale. Plain backs are simple and easy to clean. Two-tone designs, such as a coloured back with a black seat, can tie the seating to your brand. Taller backs give guests more privacy and can make a banquette the centrepiece of the room.

Materials for restaurant banquettes

For dining rooms, commercial-grade vinyl and leather are the usual choice because they wipe clean and hold up to daily use. Fabric and velvet work well in lounges and lower-traffic areas. Any colour is possible, so match your brand or your design scheme exactly.

Planning tips

  • Measure the full wall, then mark doors, vents, outlets and anything that cannot be covered
  • Decide your table sizes first, then fit the banquette to them
  • Leave a clear aisle on the chair side for servers
  • Think about how guests slide in at corners and ends
  • If you have several locations, keep one specification so every room matches

Quick answers

What is the difference between a booth and a banquette?

A booth has seating on both sides of a table, usually with backs on each side. A banquette is a long bench along a wall or around a room, with chairs on the other side of the tables.

How tall should a restaurant banquette seat be?

About 18 in (46 cm) is typical for standard 30 in (76 cm) tables. Because we build to order, we can adjust it to your tables.
