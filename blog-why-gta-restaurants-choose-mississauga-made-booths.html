Why Restaurants Across the GTA Choose Booths Made in Mississauga

By Modern Furniture Gallery, custom furniture makers in Mississauga since 2000 · November 11, 2026

If you have eaten out in the GTA, there is a good chance you have already sat in one of our booths. Since 2000, Modern Furniture Gallery has built restaurant booths, banquettes and lounge seating in our Mississauga facility for restaurants, restaurant chains, cafés, bars and lounges across Toronto and the GTA.

We believe our booths are some of the best-built in Ontario. Here is what restaurant owners tell us matters most when they choose who builds their seating, and how we build for it.

Built for your room, not a catalogue

No two dining rooms are the same. Walls are a few inches off, columns sit where you would rather they didn’t, and every concept needs a different seat count. That is why every booth we make starts with your floor plan. We build straight runs, single and double booths, L-shaped and U-shaped booths, curved banquettes, round centre-island booths and banquettes that wrap around a column, all sized to the space you actually have.

Made in Mississauga and sold factory-direct

We are the manufacturer, not a reseller. The same team that measures and quotes your project builds it in our Mississauga facility and delivers it. You get wholesaler pricing with no middleman markup, a clear answer when something needs to change, and the option to visit our showroom at 7025 Tomken Rd to see and feel the materials before you decide.

Materials that hold up to service

A booth in a busy restaurant gets slid into, spilled on and wiped down many times a day. Most of our restaurant clients choose commercial-grade vinyl or leather because it cleans easily and keeps its look. Fabric is a good fit for lounges and quieter areas. Every booth can be made in any colour to match your brand, in channel-stitch, tufted, two-tone, retro diner or plain designs.

Every location can match

For restaurant groups, consistency is everything. When we build for more than one location, we keep your sizes, materials and colours on file, so the booths in your fifth location match the first. If you open a new site or renovate an old one, we can build to the same specification.

Who we have built for

Our clients include Crab Boil, a seafood boil restaurant, Habibz Corner, a halal comfort-food restaurant with locations across the GTA, and Nikon, for whom we built commercial seating. We also build for cafés and dessert shops, banquet halls, bars, lounges and VIP areas. You can see some of this work on our restaurant projects page.

Seating you can refresh, not throw away

Good booths outlast their upholstery. When the vinyl on your booths is worn or your brand colours change, we can reupholster your existing booths in commercial-grade, easy-clean materials, which usually costs far less than replacing them.

Planning a new restaurant or a renovation?

Send us your floor plan or measurements and your opening date. We will plan the seating with you, suggest materials and give you a quote. Most custom pieces are completed within 1–3 weeks, and we deliver to restaurants across Toronto, the GTA and the rest of Ontario.
