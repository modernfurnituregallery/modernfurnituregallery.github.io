Custom Canadian-made furniture vs. imported furniture

Most furniture sold in big-box stores is mass-produced overseas in a handful of sizes and colours, then shipped across the ocean. If the size is wrong or the fabric wears out, there is often nobody local who can fix it.

Canadian-made custom furniture works the other way around. You start with your room, your style and your fabric, and we build the piece to fit. Because we manufacture in Mississauga and sell factory-direct, you skip the import markups and long shipping times, and you can see and touch every material at our showroom before you order.

That is why homeowners, interior designers and restaurant owners across Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Oakville and Vaughan choose Modern Furniture Gallery for Canadian-made sofas, sectionals, beds and custom booths.

Canadian-made furniture questions

Is your furniture made in Canada?

Yes. Every sofa, sectional, bed, accent chair, ottoman and restaurant booth we sell is custom made to order in our own workshop at 7025 Tomken Rd in Mississauga, Ontario. We do not resell imported furniture.

Is everything custom made?

Yes. Nothing is mass-produced. Each piece is built to order in the size, shape, fabric, leather and colour you choose, for homes, condos and businesses.

Where are the materials from?

Canada. Our frames, foam, fabric and leather are all sourced in Canada, and every piece is built by our own team in our Mississauga workshop, so the materials and the labour are Canadian.

Do you make Canadian-made restaurant booths?

Yes. Custom restaurant booths, banquettes and commercial seating are our specialty. Every booth is made in Canada in our Mississauga workshop and delivered to restaurants, cafés, bars and lounges across the GTA and Ontario.

How long does Canadian-made custom furniture take?

Most custom pieces are ready in about 1 to 3 weeks, much faster than furniture ordered from overseas factories. We confirm your timeline when you order.

Can I see where my furniture is made?

Yes. Visit our showroom at 7025 Tomken Rd #19 in Mississauga, open 7 days a week, to see finished pieces and choose fabrics, leathers and colours in person.

Do you deliver Canadian-made furniture outside the GTA?

Yes. We deliver across Toronto, the GTA and the rest of Ontario.
